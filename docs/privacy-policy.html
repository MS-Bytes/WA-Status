Privacy Policy

Status Saver – Story Downloader · Developer: MS-Bytes · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta. WhatsApp is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC.

1. Summary

  • All status files are processed only on your device. We never upload, collect or store your photos, videos or messages.
  • We do not require an account, login, phone number or QR scan.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data as described below.
  • We use no analytics or tracking SDKs other than Google AdMob.

2. Files and folder access

With your permission (Android Storage Access Framework), the app reads the single status folder of WhatsApp and/or WhatsApp Business that you select. It reads only image and video files already cached by those apps. Files you save are copied to Pictures/StatusSaver and Movies/StatusSaver on your device. Edited copies are also saved there. You can remove folder access at any time in the app’s Settings or in Android settings.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps your preferences (theme, auto-save, notification choices), your favorites list and, if you enable App Lock, a salted hash of your PIN. This data stays on your device. Only your preferences may be included in your personal Android backup. The PIN hash is never backed up.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free we show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android settings),
  • device information such as model, OS version, language and approximate location derived from IP address,
  • app interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Consent (GDPR, UK and US state laws)

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states) the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

6. Notifications

If you enable new-status alerts or auto-save, the app checks the selected folders on your device about every 15 minutes and may show a local notification. Nothing is sent to a server.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your files with anyone. The only data shared with third parties is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects as described in section 4.

9. Your rights and deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all data the app stored privately. Files you saved to your gallery remain until you delete them (also possible from the app’s Saved tab). For advertising data, use the Ad privacy options in the app or your Android advertising settings.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
